ICCSD General Fund Forecast

The district’s finances over time, and this model’s range of expected outcomes.

The shape of it

ICCSD runs a structural operating deficit on PFM’s numbers — expenses pull ahead of revenue — so the General Fund’s cushion stays thin and its KPIs sit below the board’s own targets through FY2029. The shaded plume shows how the range widens the further out we look: even the optimistic edge only re-enters the solvency target late, while the conservative edge goes negative.

How to read this

  • Base case is reconciled to PFM’s own 7-year General Fund cash-flow model (the district advisor’s projection).
  • The plume widens because small annual surplus/deficit differences compound on a thin fund balance — by FY2029 the outcomes range from deeply negative to a full recovery.
  • Targets are the board’s own (policy 701.5R1): solvency 10–15% (5% floor), UAB 5–10%. Both sit above where the forecast lands.
  • Debt is shown through the latest audited year (FY2023); GO bonds are being paid down while SAVE jumped on a FY2023 issuance.

Sources. data/normalized/gf_forecast.csv (this model); PFM Financial Advisors board presentations; ICCSD audited ACFRs (FY2020–2023); Iowa DOM filings. FY2024–2029 figures are unaudited estimates/projections carrying material uncertainty — that uncertainty is the plume.

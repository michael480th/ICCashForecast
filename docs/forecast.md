# ICCSD General Fund forward forecast (FY2026–FY2027)

Forecast of the General Fund and its two board-policy KPIs. Uncertain quantities are shown as a **band** across conservative / base / optimistic scenarios. KPI definitions per board policy 701.5R1.

> **Bases & caveats.** FY2024–2026 are unaudited (no ACFR issued yet). The FY2024 assigned+unassigned balance ($14.88M, FY2024 Certified Annual Report) shows the fund balance rebuilt from the FY2022–23 trough — the key driver of the forward path. The UAB projection is the most sensitive (depends on the not-yet-final authorized budget) and is modeled from ICCSD's observed spend rate; treat its band as wide.

## How the year ends — the bottom line

- **FY2026 operating result:** base **+$6.0M** (band $-0.7M to $+10.9M).
- **FY2026 assigned+unassigned fund balance:** ~$20.6M (base).
- **FY2026 solvency ratio:** base **9.8%** (band 6.8–12.1%) — recovering toward the 10–15% target but not yet inside it; above the 5% floor in the base/optimistic cases, near it in the conservative case.
- **FY2026 UAB ratio:** ~2.5% — still **below** the 5–10% target, consistent with ICCSD ranking last among large Iowa peers.

**Read:** after the FY2020–23 trough, the General Fund has been rebuilding. The base case has solvency climbing back toward — but not into — the board's target band by FY2027, while the spending-authority cushion (UAB) stays below target. The conservative band shows how a single soft revenue year (property tax / state aid timing) pulls solvency back toward the 5% floor — the core liquidity-timing risk.
